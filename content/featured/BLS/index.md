The Basic Life Support (HCP) certificate is crucial for nurses as it equips them with essential skills to respond swiftly and effectively during life-threatening emergencies. It enables nurses to perform critical interventions such as CPR and use of defibrillators, potentially saving lives in various healthcare settings. This certification ensures nurses are prepared to initiate immediate care, maintaining vital functions until advanced medical help arrives, significantly improving patient outcomes in critical situations.
